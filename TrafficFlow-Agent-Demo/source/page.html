    Project architecture, assumptions & demo policy

    Mapping to your challenge brief

    • Multi-modal signals: structured simulated CCTV events, vehicle-to-infrastructure reports, emergency requests, road state, signal plans and weather.
    • Detection and prediction: evidence fusion plus a finite-storage queue simulation across J1, J2 and J3.
    • Intervention: signal timing proposals, obstruction protection, emergency corridor planning and demand diversion.
    • Validation: each action passes or fails explicit rules before the simulator can apply it.
    • Coordination: response tasks and operator acknowledgements with a local event log.
    • Explanation: separate operator and public drafts tied to the scenario.

    Illustrative guardrails, not traffic regulations

    120s cycle; main green at most 65s; pedestrian interval at least 15s; clearance at least 4s; other movements at least 25s; observations at most 60s old. Emergency scenarios also require authenticated requests, at least 150m visibility and an available team. Obstruction protection must retain one passable lane.

    The agents are separate deterministic software modules, not independently trained or LLM-driven agents. Camera confidence and authentication are demo inputs, not computer vision or cryptographic verification.

    The queue model assumes 30 vehicles/min per open lane at continuous green, adjusts capacity for weather and signal share, and limits downstream storage. Diversion reduces arrivals by a fixed 12%. Simulated response clears an obstruction after 6 minutes with approved protection or 10 minutes with dispatch only. These are unvalidated scenario assumptions.

    Waiting vehicle-minutes are an idling proxy. This demo does not calculate emissions, injury risk, real emergency ETA or proven clearance improvements. A live deployment requires validated sensor models, local engineering rules, secure V2X and authorized infrastructure integrations.
